# Candidates — Make the AI Judge Laugh (u-rama-melon)

Bounty: one funny line about office life, 1–200 characters, judged pairwise
by an AI judge against this brief, criteria in order:

1. **On-topic & lands** — about office life and lands as a joke.
2. **True observation** — meetings, email, bosses, coffee, deadlines, the
   printer; the listener nods before laughing.
3. **Surprising turn** — a fresh angle or an unexpected last word, not a
   pun or a stock line.
4. **Tight** — one line, no wasted setup, reads cleanly aloud in one breath.
5. **Original** — not a familiar line with the nouns swapped.

Every candidate below is scored 1–5 on each criterion, in that order
(C1/C2/C3/C4/C5), total /25. Candidates were drafted across seven devices:
reframe, versus-frame, concrete scene, escalation, personification,
self-own, and deadpan report. An 8-seed knockout among the top scorers
decides the winner; ties in the knockout are broken at the earliest
criterion where the two lines differ, exactly as the brief instructs.
